## Invariants

- tenant and organization boundaries are explicit;
- permission evidence is synchronized from governed sources;
- authorization filtering happens before content reaches the reader;
- unauthorized and missing resources do not leak existence.

<VerificationBlock title="Required evidence">
  Claims are traced to the authorization model, service checks, and negative
